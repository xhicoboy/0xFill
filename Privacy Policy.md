# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the **0xFill - Quick Fill** browser extension (the "Extension") created by hico (the "Service Provider"). The Extension is provided as a free service and is intended for use "AS IS".

**Last updated:** 2026-03-10

## What the Extension Does

0xFill helps you quickly insert local test data (such as random emails, sample phone numbers, random text, and custom snippets) into editable fields on web pages through the browser context menu.

## Information Collection

The Extension **does not collect, store remotely, transmit, or sell** personal data.

- It does **not** collect IP addresses, browsing history, form contents, or analytics.
- It does **not** use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to process your data.
- It does **not** use third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics SDKs.
- Fill actions happen **locally in your browser**. Generated test values are not uploaded to the Service Provider.

## Local Storage

Optional settings you configure (for example: email prefix/domain and custom snippets) are saved using the browser's `chrome.storage.sync` API so they can sync with your browser profile. This data stays within your browser account/storage and is not sent to the Service Provider's servers.

## Permissions

The Extension may request permissions required for its core features, such as:

- showing context menu items on web pages
- reading/writing Extension settings in browser storage
- locating frames in a tab so fill actions can work inside iframes
- injecting/running a content script on pages where you choose to fill data

These permissions are used only to provide the fill feature you trigger. The Extension does not use them to monitor your browsing.

## Children's Privacy

The Extension is not directed to children under 13, and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted by updating this document. Continued use of the Extension after updates means you accept the revised policy.
