    03

    How to use

    Fill data from the context menu without leaving the page.

    1. Right-click anywhere on a page and select “0xFill - Quick Fill.”
    2. “One Click Fill” finds the form near where you right-clicked and skips passwords, verification codes, and card numbers.
    3. Email addresses and phone numbers replace the entire field by default; selected text is replaced instead.
    4. Random text is inserted at the cursor without clearing the field.
    5. Custom menus support up to three levels: parent menu → child item or submenu → nested item.
    6. Click the extension icon anytime to open this settings page.
    Save your changes to sync them with the context menu.
